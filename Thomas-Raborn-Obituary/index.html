In Loving Memory

Thomas Philip Raborn

April 4th, 1938 – June 25th, 2026

Thomas Philip Raborn has gone home to be with the Lord.

On June 25th, 2026, at the age of 88, Tom left this world to be with the Savior he loved above everything else. His faith was not one part of his life among many. It was the foundation beneath all of it, the quiet center from which everything else in him grew.

He was born on April 4th, 1938, in Bude, Mississippi, and raised in the small town of Osyka, where he grew up and graduated from Osyka High School. A gifted athlete from his earliest years, Tom loved sports and loved competition, and his talent on the basketball court earned him a scholarship to Louisiana State University. Years of hard work in Baton Rouge paid off when in 1959 he was recognized as the MVP of the LSU men’s basketball team.

At LSU he also built the foundation of his career, earning both a bachelor’s degree and a master’s degree in mathematics. That love of numbers led him to a long career as an actuary, a profession perfectly suited to a mind as precise and disciplined as his. His work took him and his family across the South, from Louisiana to Florida then to Mississippi, before he settled in Carmel, Indiana, in 1995, where he would spend the rest of his life.

Anyone who knew Tom knew a kind man of integrity with a servant’s heart who was always willing to lend a hand. He was also, without exaggeration, one of the cleanest and most organized people you could ever hope to meet. He kept his belongings in their original packaging and could produce the instruction manual for something he had purchased forty years earlier. His financial records were legendary, with ledgers reaching back decades. He did everything with care and thoroughness, and he brought that same faithfulness to every corner of his life.

In Carmel he was a devoted and active member of the Carmel Seventh-day Adventist Church, where his steady, humble faith was a blessing to all who worshiped alongside him. An athlete to the very end, he channeled his lifelong love of competition into tennis, becoming an avid player who simply loved the game.

To his grandchildren and great-grandchildren, he was affectionately known as Paw Paw Tom, a name spoken with love by the family that meant the world to him.

Tom was preceded in death by his parents, Marvin Hodges Raborn and Velma McMillian Raborn Addison, and by his brother, James Edward Raborn.

He is survived by his sister, Kathryn Raborn Finch of Covington, Louisiana; his sons, Richard “Rick” Raborn (Virginia) and Tommy Raborn; his grandchildren, Tyler Raborn (Amanda), Casey Boothe (Spencer), Claire Tapley, Emily Tapley, and London Webb (Garrett); and his six cherished great-grandchildren, Addie Marie Boothe, Scottie Boothe, Ann Brees Raborn, Breck Raborn, Isla Claire Raborn, and Hart Raborn.

Above all else, Tom loved the Lord, and it is in that love that his family finds its comfort now, knowing that the faith which shaped his every day has carried him home at last.

In lieu of flowers, memorial donations may be made in his memory to the
