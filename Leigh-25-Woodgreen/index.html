    How the pricing works

    Everything is included. Rates are full-service: utilities, Wi-Fi, property taxes, building insurance, cleaning of shared areas, lawn care, pest control, security and maintenance are all inside the number.

    Founding member pricing. Agreements signed during the building's founding period get 15% off the standard rate, locked in for the full first term. Renewals price at the standard rate in effect at the time.

    Furnished or unfurnished. Furnished offices come ready to work in and run 20% above the unfurnished rate. The lower-level flex and storage rooms are offered unfurnished only.

    Terms run 12, 18, 24, 30 or 36 months. Two discounts stack on the founding rate. Each office after the first takes 1% off, up to 10%; the add-on room doesn't count toward that tally, though it still gets founding and term pricing. Longer terms take 1.5% off for every 6 months past 12, up to 6% at 36 months. The builder applies all of it automatically.

    Month-to-month is the flexible option: no commitment, at 25% above the founding 12-month rate.
