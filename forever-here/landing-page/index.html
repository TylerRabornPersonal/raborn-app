How it works

Three simple steps. The hard part, showing up, we handle.

1

Record your voice

Say what you'd want them to hear. Take your time, listen back, and re-record until it's right.

2

Choose when it arrives

Pick a date, a milestone like a wedding or 40th birthday, or set it to be delivered someday when you're gone.

3

It arrives as a call

On the day, their phone rings and your message plays. The moment feels like you reaching out, because it is.
