1.0The starting point

Foundation.

Forever Here records personal voice and video messages and delivers them on a chosen date and time, including after the sender has passed.

Brand promise

Stay connected forever.

The differentiator is arrival. Messages are intended to reach the recipient from the sender's verified phone number, so the moment feels familiar and personal rather than administrative.

Emotional tone

Compassionate, reassuring, premium, intimate, hopeful, respectful. Never morbid, frightening, or overly sentimental.

Visual tone

Cinematic dusk imagery, deep navy and black surfaces, glowing fire-and-ice accents, restrained glass effects, spacious editorial typography.

Presence, not storage

We are not a vault someone has to remember to open. The phone rings. Every design decision should serve that arrival.

Warmth with dignity

We work in love, grief, and legacy. Hopeful and human, never a gimmick, never fear-based.

Trust by design

People hand us the most important thing they will ever say. Verification and safeguards are the reason anyone believes us.

5.0Photography & visual direction

Imagery.Licensing required

Cinematic waterfront at dusk. Warm orange sunset at the horizon, deep navy shadows, loved ones in silhouette. The focus is connection and presence, never grief imagery.

Do Cinematic lake or waterfront at dusk. Warm sunset strip preserved to support the fire side of the brand. Dark navy overlay so text meets contrast. Soft grain, restrained bloom. Phone UI stays crisp while the background stays cinematic.
Do not No cemetery or hospital imagery, visible distress, or literal depictions of death. No heavy HDR. Keep faces non-specific unless model releases are obtained.
Phone mockup rules
  • Build the phone and call UI as separate responsive assets or HTML/CSS layers, never cropped from the mockup image.
  • The contact name and photo must remain editable.
  • The screen must not imply a live call when the experience is a scheduled recording.
  • Include a label such as Scheduled message if required by legal or product review.
  • Do not crop so tightly that the call state becomes unclear.

9.0Motion

Movement should feel like a breath.

Ambient only

Logo glow may gently breathe over 5–8 seconds. Hero background may use a very small scale or parallax shift.

Entrances

The phone enters with a short fade and slight vertical movement. Nothing bounces or springs.

Never urgent

Avoid ringing animations that feel alarming. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Never hide essential content behind animation.

10.0A baseline, not a checkbox

Accessibility.

Our audiences include the grieving and the elderly. Clarity is compassion. Target WCAG 2.2 AA.

  • 4.5:1 contrast for body text, 3:1 for large text.
  • Never use color alone to distinguish fire vs. ice states or form errors.
  • Every field has a persistent visible label and an associated error message. Placeholders are not labels.
  • All CTAs and dropdowns operable by keyboard, with visible focus styles (2–3px outline, not glow alone).
  • Captions and transcripts for every voice and video example.
  • Tap targets at least 44 × 44px.
  • Semantic landmarks: header, nav, main, section, footer.
  • Errors use a dedicated accessible red, never Fire Orange alone.
11.0Before launch

Product claims requiring review.Legal

These five claims appear in the marketing and must be confirmed by technical and legal review before they ship.

"Delivered from your actual phone number"

Verify caller ID, carrier, verification, consent, and regional compliance. If the implementation uses a proxy or branded number, the copy must be revised.

"Private. Secure. Encrypted."

State whether data is encrypted in transit, at rest, or end to end. Do not claim end-to-end unless technically accurate.

Delivery after death

Define identity verification, account stewardship, cancellation rights, authorized contacts, delivery triggers, and data retention.

Emotional transparency

The interface must make clear the recipient is hearing a previously recorded message, not a live call.
