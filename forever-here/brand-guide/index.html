1.0 The starting point

Foundation. What Forever Here exists to do.

We turn a recorded message into a phone call that arrives on the exact day it means the most, even years from now, even after someone is gone.

Mission

A father records a message today. On his daughter's wedding day, her phone rings and it is his voice. Forever Here makes sure the people we love can still show up for the moments that matter.

Why it is different

Other services store files in a vault that someone has to remember to open. Forever Here does not wait to be opened. It rings. A scheduled message becomes a real, incoming phone call, introduced gently, delivered on a date, a milestone, or upon a verified passing.

That is the whole promise: presence, not preservation.

Pillars

Three commitments that shape every decision.

Presence over preservation

We are not a storage locker for memories. We are the moment a phone rings and a voice is there. Every design choice should serve that arrival, and make it feel real.

Emotion with dignity

We work in the territory of love, grief, and legacy. We are warm and hopeful, never morbid, never a gimmick. We treat a person's last words with the care they deserve.

Trust by design

People hand us the most important thing they will ever say. Verified identity, secure storage, and guardian confirmation are not features. They are the reason anyone believes us.

The test

Before anything ships, read it aloud as if you were speaking to someone who just lost a parent.

If it would sound cold, cute, or salesy in that room, rewrite it. Sincerity is the brand's whole competitive edge. Protect it in every line of copy.

3.0 Who we serve

Four audiences, one brand.

The same promise reaches very different people. The voice stays constant; the emphasis shifts.

01
Primary early market

First responders & military

People who run toward danger and want a safeguard for the family they might not come home to. The pitch is protection and peace of mind. Sober, respectful, never fear-mongering.

02
Everyday heart of the product

Families & individuals

Parents, spouses, grandparents. Messages for weddings, birthdays, graduations, and the ordinary "just wanted to hear my voice." Warm, hopeful, celebratory.

03
End-of-life & legacy

Aging, hospice & care partners

Seniors, terminally ill patients, and the nursing homes, hospitals, and hospice teams who serve them. The most tender context we work in. Gentle, unhurried, dignified.

04
Channel & partners

Institutions & sponsors

Departments, unions, funeral homes, life insurers, and faith communities who offer Forever Here to the people they serve. Credible, clear, trustworthy. This is where B2B commitments come from.

7.0 Rhythm

Spacing & layout.

A 4px base unit keeps app and web on the same grid. Generous whitespace is part of the reverence; do not crowd tender moments.

Rules of thumb

Touch targets never below 44px tall on mobile. Buttons and inputs get real breathing room.

Screens use 20–24px side gutters on mobile, up to 32px on web.

Content width caps around 860px for reading so long, emotional copy stays comfortable.

Radius is soft but not bubbly: pill buttons, 16px cards, 24px sheets.

10.0 Imagery

Photography & illustration. Real, tender, unstaged.

Faces and hands in genuine moments. Warm light. Fire and ice appear as light and atmosphere, not literal flames and ice cubes.

DoA parent and child, real light, a phone in hand at a real moment
AvoidStocky, staged smiles, literal grim-reaper or graveyard clichés, cold tech imagery
Light

Golden-hour warmth against cool shadow. The fire/ice duality lives in the lighting, subtly.

Subjects

First responders with family, weddings, graduations, grandparents on the phone. Dignity always.

Restraint

Grief is implied, never exploited. No tears-on-cue, no fear imagery, no coffins.

Rob's concept art (the fire/ice heart-cloud splash and the campaign imagery) sets the target mood. Final licensed or original photography drops into these slots.

11.0 Motion

Motion. Gentle and intentional.

Movement should feel like a breath, not a bounce. Ease out, never spring. The incoming call is the one moment allowed a little drama.

Fast · 160ms

Taps, toggles, button presses.

Base · 260ms

Screen transitions, sheets, cards.

Slow · 460ms

The call arriving, the message revealing.

Respect prefers-reduced-motion. When set, transitions cap at 80ms or turn off. Nothing critical relies on motion.

13.0 A baseline, not a checkbox

Accessibility.

Our audiences include the grieving, the elderly, and people in hard moments. Clarity is compassion. Target WCAG 2.2 AA at minimum.

✓

Color contrast

4.5:1 for body, 3:1 for large text. Use only the verified pairings in Section 5.0. Never signal state with color alone.

✓

Touch targets

44 × 44px minimum on mobile. The record and call buttons are large and forgiving by design.

✓

Reading level

Plain language, short sentences. Someone reading through tears should understand it on the first pass.

✓

Forms

Visible labels, marked required fields, inline errors announced via aria-live, never placeholder-only.

✓

Reduced motion

Honor prefers-reduced-motion. No autoplay of the call animation when it is set.

✓

Captions

Every delivered voice message offers a text transcript, for accessibility and for keepsake.
