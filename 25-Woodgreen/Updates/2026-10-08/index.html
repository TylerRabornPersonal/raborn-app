Building updates for October

Hi y’all!

For those of you I haven’t met, my name is Amanda Raborn, and I will be your point of contact for all things 25 Woodgreen from the Raborn Realty side.

Here’s our first building update. Going forward, you’ll get these emails periodically so you know what’s happening at 25 Woodgreen and when.

Updates
This week

Demo work begins. Crews will be on site starting this week.

Parking. We’ve asked the crews not to park in the two spaces closest to the entrance stairs. Please let us know if this becomes an issue.

Glass doors. Work is underway to install glass doors that will separate the Hood Packaging suite area from the rest of the building.
Friday, October 9th, then every Saturday

Weekly cleaning begins. This week the crew will clean on Friday. After that, they’ll clean the common areas and restrooms and take the trash out to the street every Saturday. The crew is not allowed to enter any private office or suite.
Throughout October

Internet and Wi-Fi. Fiber internet is being installed this month, and our IT firm, Raborn IT, will install Wi-Fi access points throughout the building. Hood Packaging will remain on its own internet service, but the building Wi-Fi will be available to anyone who wants to use it.
Coming weeks

Touch-ups. Crews will do finish and touch-up work on the 2001 building’s main floor and basement.
End of October

Access control goes live, including mobile entry to the building. Our system will control the building’s exterior entry doors. Hood Packaging will install and manage access control for its own suite. As we lease additional suites, each office will have its own lock.

What we’re working on

On-site property management contact. Raborn Realty is hiring a dedicated point of contact for property management. This person will work from the building three to four days a week and handle day-to-day building needs. We’ll introduce them as soon as the position is filled.

Trash. We’re putting a consistent trash routine in place. The cleaning crew will take trash to the street every Saturday. Once our property management contact is on board, they’ll also collect common-area trash during the week, take it out to the street, and bring the cans back up after pickup. We’re also working on a location for a trash receptacle.

Thank you for your patience while we make these improvements. If you have questions, reply to this email or call me at 601-624-3494.

Talk soon!

Amanda Raborn
