SpecCraft · Pricing and Revenue Models

Standard Pricing and Revenue Modeling

Subscription by seat · monthly or annual · scaled initiation fee · 20% referral commission

What SpecCraft is (and how it compares)

SpecCraft is the spec assembly and production engine that gives civil firms back the retired MasterWorks and SpecText workflow. It is web based, single tenant, and owned by the firm. It organizes the firm’s own master library into CSI MasterFormat divisions and outputs a finished Word and PDF project manual with an automatically built table of contents and consistent page numbering.

Key difference vs. SpecLink and Deltek Specpoint
Those tools bundle a maintained commercial content library plus authoring software, and a large share of their price is that content. SpecCraft is the software layer for firms that already own a mature master library, so the per seat comparison holds, and the firm keeps the content it spent decades building. The scaled initiation fee captures the real, differentiated work of migrating that library in. (MasterFormat is a standard that SpecCraft leverages, not a competitor.)

Billing and discount logic
Annual equals 10 times the monthly rate, so a year works out to paying for 10 months and getting 12. Monthly stays available for firms that want to go month to month; annual is billed upfront and lowers churn.
Referral commission
20% of seat revenue, every year, for the life of the account, paid on whatever seat revenue is invoiced. It excludes the initiation fee.

Seat changes partway through the term

Adding a seat

Monthly Added to the next invoice at the tier rate, with no proration.

Annual The seat shares the account’s renewal date. You charge only for the remaining whole months, then it renews alongside every other seat. For example, with 5 months left on the 6 to 14 tier, that is 5/12 of $720, or $300 now.

When a seat crosses into the next tier

The new seat gets the lower tier rate right away. The existing seats move down to the new rate at their next renewal (annual) or next monthly cycle (monthly), with no retroactive credits partway through the term. For example, a firm with 5 seats at $840 that adds a 6th: the 6th is priced at $720, and the other five move to $720 at renewal.
