    How the pricing works

    Everything is included. Rates are full-service license fees: utilities, property taxes, building insurance, lawn care, pest control, security, and maintenance are all inside the number.

    Founding member pricing. Agreements signed during the building's founding period get 15% off the standard rate card, locked in for the full length of the initial term. Renewals price at the then-current standard card.

    Terms run 12, 18, 24, 30, or 36 months. Two more discounts stack on top of the founding rate: one for the number of offices you take (the closet and the common area don't count toward the office tally, though they still get founding and term pricing), and one for the length of term. More offices and a longer term both move the rate down, and the builder above applies everything automatically.

    Month-to-month is the flexible option: no commitment, priced at a premium to the 12-month rate. Any room that stays in use, whether that's people, files, furniture, or equipment, bills at the month-to-month rate until it's under a term or released broom-clean.
