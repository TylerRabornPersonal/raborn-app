Please read and initial

This is a legal agreement. Initial each section to show you've read it. In this agreement, "Massey's Manor" means Massey's Manor, the landowner Mike Massey, and their family members, employees, volunteers, and agents (the "Released Parties").

1.

Required Mississippi notice

WARNING

Under Mississippi law, there is no liability for an injury to or death of a participant in an agritourism activity conducted at this agritourism location if such injury or death results from the inherent risks of the agritourism activity. Inherent risks of agritourism activities include, among others, risks of injury inherent to land, equipment, and animals, as well as the potential for you to act in a negligent manner that may contribute to your injury or death. You are assuming the risk of participating in this agritourism activity.

2.

Activities and their risks

Visiting a working farm and pumpkin patch involves real risks that can't be fully removed, even with reasonable care. They include, but aren't limited to:

  • Uneven ground, holes, mud, vines, stems, rocks, and slippery or wet surfaces
  • Hayrides, tractors, wagons, and other farm equipment, including sudden stops and movement
  • Farm animals, which can bite, kick, scratch, or carry germs
  • Insects, spiders, snakes, and other wildlife
  • Weather, heat, sun, and cold
  • Hay, straw, plants, pollen, and dust that can cause allergic reactions or irritation
  • Hay bales, play areas, mazes, and other attractions
  • Lifting and carrying pumpkins, and the tools used to cut them
  • Vehicles in parking and drop-off areas
  • The actions of other visitors, and your own or your children's actions

These risks can cause injuries ranging from minor scrapes to serious injury, illness, or death, and damage to personal property.

3.

Assumption of risk

I am choosing to visit Massey's Manor and take part in its activities voluntarily. I knowingly assume all risks of visiting and participating, both known and unknown, for myself and for any child listed on this waiver, even if those risks come from the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties.

4.

Release of claims and indemnity

To the fullest extent allowed by law, I release, waive, and agree not to sue the Released Parties for any injury, illness, death, or property damage to me or any child listed on this waiver arising from my visit, including claims caused by the ordinary negligence of the Released Parties.

I also agree to indemnify and hold harmless the Released Parties from any claim, cost, or expense, including attorney's fees, brought by me, any child listed on this waiver, or anyone acting on our behalf. This release does not cover gross negligence or intentional misconduct by the Released Parties.

5.

Children in my care

I confirm I am the parent or legal guardian of each child listed on this waiver, or have their parent or guardian's permission to bring them and sign for them. I give permission for them to take part, I agree to the terms of this waiver on their behalf, and I will supervise them at all times while on the property.

5.

Patch rules

  • Stay on marked paths and out of closed or roped-off areas.
  • Keep children within sight and reach at all times.
  • Stay seated on hayrides with hands and feet inside the wagon.
  • Don't climb on tractors, equipment, fences, or stacked hay unless it's marked for play.
  • Wash hands after touching animals. Don't feed them anything you didn't get from us.
  • No smoking, vaping, alcohol, or fireworks. Service animals only.
  • Follow staff instructions. We may ask anyone not following the rules to leave.

6.

Medical care

If I or a child listed on this waiver needs medical attention and I can't be reached, I authorize the Released Parties to arrange first aid and emergency medical care. I'm responsible for any costs of that care.
